# CareLoop AI — 3-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

**Screen:** Public showcase.

**Narration:**

> A medical consultation does not end when the patient leaves. Recommendations may be forgotten, symptoms may change, and patients may need another appointment before anyone from the clinic knows. CareLoop AI turns consented follow-up phone calls into structured care actions.

## 0:20–0:45 — The workflow

**Screen:** Scroll through “One continuous post-care workflow.”

> A clinician records recommendations, staff schedules a follow-up, CALL-E contacts the patient, and the outcome becomes part of the clinic workflow. CareLoop collects patient-reported information; it never diagnoses or prescribes.

## 0:45–1:15 — Consultation and scheduling

**Screen:** Sign in, open a fictional patient, show the consultation and schedule a follow-up.

> Here is a fictional patient timeline. The clinical guidance comes from the doctor. Staff can schedule outreach only when automated-call consent is recorded.

## 1:15–1:55 — CALL-E follow-up

**Screen:** Follow-ups. In the final recording use live CALL-E mode and an authorized test number. Until access is restored, use the Clinical alert scenario.

> CareLoop sends CALL-E a safety-bounded task and a strict structured-result schema. The call identifies itself as automated and asks about recommendation follow-through, evolution, new symptoms, appointment needs, and care rating.

> During development we can safely demonstrate the same downstream workflow without contacting a person. For the final demo, this section will show the authorized live CALL-E call.

## 1:55–2:30 — Result and human review

**Screen:** Return to the patient timeline, open the transcript, then mark the alert reviewed.

> The result is not left in a call log. It updates the patient timeline with the summary, transcript, structured outcome, rating, and appointment request. A new or worsening symptom creates a human-review alert. Clinic staff remains responsible for every clinical decision.

## 2:30–2:50 — Dashboard

**Screen:** Dashboard and Insights.

> The dashboard shows reach, completion, ratings, appointments, and unresolved reviews, helping the clinic understand its post-care workload.

## 2:50–3:00 — Close

**Screen:** Public showcase hero.

> CareLoop AI closes the gap between a consultation and the next human decision: consent-first, structured, and connected through CALL-E.

## Recording checklist

- Replace the mock segment with one authorized live CALL-E call before final submission.
- Keep the video between 2:45 and 3:10.
- Use fictional patient records and hide all API keys, emails, phone numbers, and browser bookmarks.
- Record at 1920 × 1080 and zoom the browser to 100%.
- Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted**, not Private.
